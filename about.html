01 / Why THiNK is Different

Built by operators,
for operators.

THiNK is powered by PAN, a group built by operators with direct experience across business transformation, turnarounds, investment, innovation, start ups, operating performance and high-performance environments.

02 / What THiNK Does

Empowering from the inside-out.

THiNK helps businesses build the thinking capability required to perform at a higher level.

Our approach is to empower from the inside out. We do not arrive with a predetermined answer. We ask the questions that help people inside the organisation examine the business differently.

Leaders understand the market, the customer, the team and the operating model. The challenge is that insight can become fragmented across the organisation, shaped by existing assumptions or lost in the pressure of day-to-day execution.

THiNK creates the structure and environment to bring that thinking together. We add an objective perspective shaped by operating experience, helping leadership teams challenge the current view, develop stronger ideas and focus on the areas most likely to improve performance.

From there, the work moves into action. Strategic intent is translated into clear priorities and tangible plans that the business can implement and carry forward.

04 / Case Studies

How THiNK has empowered high performance.

01

Global Beverage Company

A high-pressure business transformation involving significant debt, operating complexity and the need to restore performance. The business was carrying approximately $700 million in debt before being turned around and ultimately sold for approximately $1.5 billion.

THiNK Relevance

This business reflects the kind of thinking THiNK brings to businesses under pressure: clarity on the current reality, sharper priorities, disciplined execution and the ability to convert constraint into commercial momentum.

02

Fast-Moving Consumer Goods

A fast-moving consumer goods turnaround where the business was operating under material debt pressure and needed a clear path to improved performance. The business was carrying approximately $70 million in debt and moved through turnaround toward an approximate $100 million sale.

THiNK Relevance

THiNK’s ability to work through complexity, identify the issues that matter, and help businesses move from pressure to a clearer performance pathway.

03

Professional Sporting Club

A professional sporting environment requiring financial reset, cultural change and performance improvement. The broader transformation included the establishment of a future fund, improved profitability, a Grand Final appearance and, ultimately, a Premiership.

THiNK Relevance

This case reflects a core THiNK belief: high performance is created through the environment around people.

04

Global Movement Impacting Global Poverty

A global movement built around the ambition to end extreme poverty, mobilising people, partners, capital and attention around a shared mission. The work required the ability to convert broad support into coordinated action at scale, align diverse stakeholders and maintain momentum across a complex global network.

THiNK Relevance

THiNK’s focus on creating alignment around a clear objective and translating collective intent into meaningful action. It demonstrates what becomes possible when people understand why the organisation exists and work in alignment toward a shared outcome.

05

Australia’s Largest Training Business

A business acquired out of administration, requiring stabilisation, commercial discipline and a pathway to growth, including acquiring another company, merging business units and leading a technology transformation.

THiNK Relevance

THiNK’s focus on converting complexity into clarity, especially when a business needs to stabilise, integrate, transform and build toward long-term value.

06

Idea Incubation Impacting Health Care

The incubation of Care Guidance from concept, to in-market, to scaling across Australia, building an early-stage idea into a platform capable of creating meaningful change in the community.

THiNK Relevance

This case reflects the full THiNK pathway: insights, ideas and innovation, unlocking new opportunities and turning early potential into practical outcomes.

This experience shapes how we work and how we think about business. We bring commercial judgement, thoughtful questions, performance discipline and a practical understanding of what it takes to move a business from potential to improved performance.

We are not interested in strategy that sits outside the operating reality of the business. We are interested in the thinking that changes what the business does next.

05 / Who THiNK Works With

Ambitious businesses, across every industry.

THiNK works with ambitious businesses across a range of industries. The operating context may differ, but the underlying performance challenges are often familiar: a lack of prioritisation, inconsistent decision-making, misalignment and difficulty translating strategy into action.

THiNK is most valuable when a business has strong potential but needs greater clarity around its direction and more discipline in execution to convert that potential into performance.
